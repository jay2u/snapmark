# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

Snapmark does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. Everything happens inside your browser.

## What the extension handles
- **Screenshots and annotations** you create. They are stored in your browser's local storage
  (IndexedDB) so you can reopen and edit them from the History page. You can delete any capture, or
  all of them, from that page. Uninstalling the extension removes them.
- **Settings** (colours, sizes, file format, history limit) stored with `chrome.storage.local`.

## What the extension does not do
- It makes **no network requests** and has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It does not read or record anything on a page until **you** start a capture.
- It does not upload screenshots anywhere. Copy and Download only act on your device.
